\section{Equal-DOF baseline comparisons}
\label{sec:results}

\subsection{Comparison setup and scope}

Five cases use a common evaluator: linear and cubic in one dimension, sinh
in two dimensions, and pure \(p=4\) in one and two dimensions. Counts are
retained CGA/RFM coefficients and independent FEM coefficients after removing
the constant mode where required; equal counts imply neither equal numerical
rank nor equal computational work.

The \texttt{review\_replay\_20261001} batch refits coefficients on fixed
archived CGA greedy prefixes, separately from the original training trajectories
in \Cref{sec:numerics}. FEM uses uniform one-dimensional or structured triangular
meshes with P1/P3 references and supplementary P2 states. RFM uses nested
normalized \(\relu^3\) ridges and ten prespecified seeds, 201--210, per width.
The archive has 320 RFM, 32 CGA-prefix, and 114 FEM states, including failures.
Sampling, fitting, quadrature, and audit settings are frozen in the protocol
specified in S4. Historical baseline statistics are retained separately.

Figures join computed dyadic states and terminal CGA prefixes; FEM uses its
mesh counts. Display caps are the archived CGA terminal widths.
Table endpoints are fixed at \(256,256,256,128,256\) for C1--C5.
FEM tables interpolate log(error) against log(DOF) between metric-valid states,
without extrapolation; P3 meshes do not bracket 512 in two dimensions.
These count-based choices precede inspection of the reported errors. Figures
contain neither common-grid interpolants nor theoretical reference lines.

\subsection{Evaluation and statistical samples}

Every method uses the same evaluator and metric-specific rule: a successful
solve, a finite nonnegative metric, and successive permitted evaluation-quadrature
relative difference at most one percent. Signed negative energy gaps remain
raw and do not enter energy summaries; an energy exclusion does not itself
exclude a stable Sobolev or \(V\) observation. Quadrature agreement is a
numerical diagnostic, not a rigorous enclosure.

RFM medians and interquartile ranges use each metric's valid observations.
The denominator remains ten; \emph{Valid} in \Cref{tab:baseline-terminal}
counts its Sobolev sample. Incomplete samples are conditional comparisons.
S3 supplies solve/audit reasons, sample counts, and model hashes.

\Cref{tab:baseline-terminal} reports endpoint values and ratios.
A ratio above one favors CGA at that count; a ratio below one favors the
comparison method. Rankings depend on the problem, count, evaluation policy,
and valid sample. These data establish neither uniform dominance at every
DOF nor a ranking over excluded realizations.
For the one-dimensional pure \(p=4\) case at \(N=128\), the
P3/CGA ratio is about 0.88 and the RFM-median/CGA ratio is about 2.30.
Thus the interpolated P3 reference has a slightly smaller error than CGA
at this endpoint, while CGA is more accurate than the specified RFM median.

\subsection{Interpretation limits}

These comparisons cover the specified archived fits, without establishing
runtime ordering, asymptotic complexity, or rankings over excluded realizations.
Energy gaps may remain unresolved while another metric is stable. S3--S4 give
solve statuses, audit exclusions, interpolation brackets, and coefficient
models supporting the figures and tables.
